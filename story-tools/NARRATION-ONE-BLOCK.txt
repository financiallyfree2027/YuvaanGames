Moon Rescue.
Milo put on his space suit.
“Are you ready, Robo?” he asked.
“Ready!” said Robo.
Their rocket flew up into the sky.
Soon, they landed on the Moon.
Milo jumped.
“Wow! I can jump so high!”
Robo said, “The Moon has less gravity than Earth.”
Milo looked around.
He saw rocks and big holes on the ground.
Then Robo pointed ahead.
“There is the Moon Rover!”
The rover was stuck inside a deep hole.
“We have to help it,” said Milo.
Milo tied a rope to the rover.
“One, two, three… pull!”
They pulled it out.
“We did it!” shouted Milo.
Before going home, Milo looked at Earth.
“It looks so beautiful from here.”

Journey Inside the Body.
One morning, Robo pressed a strange button.
ZAP!
Robo became very small.
Then Milo became small too.
“Where are we going?” asked Milo.
“Inside your body!” said Robo.
WHOOSH!
Soon they heard a loud sound.
BOOM-BOOM. BOOM-BOOM.
“What is that?” asked Milo.
“Your heart,” said Robo.
“The heart pumps blood around your body.”
Next, they saw two big pink parts.
“What are those?”
“Your lungs. They help you breathe.”
Then they travelled up to the brain.
“This is your brain,” said Robo.
“It helps you think, learn, and remember.”
Milo smiled.
“My body is amazing!”

Dinosaur Island.
Milo and Robo landed on a strange island.
THUMP!
THUMP!
THUMP!
The ground began to shake.
“What was that?” whispered Milo.
A huge dinosaur walked toward them.
Milo quickly hid behind Robo.
But the dinosaur did not chase them.
It started eating leaves.
Robo smiled.
“This dinosaur eats plants.”
Suddenly—
ROAAAAR!
Another dinosaur came running.
It had big, sharp teeth.
“That one eats meat!” shouted Robo.
“Run!”
They hid behind a large rock.
Milo looked down.
He saw something inside the rock.
“What is this?”
“It is a fossil,” said Robo.
“Fossils help us learn about animals that lived long ago.”
Milo smiled.
“Dinosaurs lived on Earth before people!”

The Secret Ant City.
Milo saw a tiny ant carrying bread.
“Look, Robo! That bread is bigger than the ant!”
“Let's follow her,” said Robo.
The ant walked into a little hole.
Milo and Robo became tiny and followed her.
Inside, they found a huge ant city.
“Wow!” said Milo.
There were ants everywhere.
Some carried food.
Some looked after baby ants.
Some cleaned the home.
“They are all working together,” said Milo.
Then they saw a large ant.
“That is the queen ant,” said Robo.
Suddenly, a piece of food fell outside.
Many ants came together.
They carried it back home.
Milo smiled.
“One ant is small, but many ants are strong!”

Ocean Rescue.
Milo and Robo went under the sea in a little submarine.
They saw colourful fish.
They saw a turtle.
They even saw a starfish.
Then they heard a strange sound.
“Ooooooo!”
“What was that?” asked Milo.
“A whale!” said Robo.
They followed the sound.
A baby whale was stuck in a net.
“Oh no!” said Milo.
“We have to help.”
Milo carefully cut the net.
The baby whale was free.
It quickly started swimming up.
“Where is it going?” asked Milo.
“To breathe,” said Robo.
“Whales need air, just like us.”
The whale reached the top of the water.
WHOOSH!
It took a big breath.
Milo waved.
“Goodbye, little whale!”

The Missing Rainbow.
It rained all morning.
Then the rain stopped.
Milo ran outside.
“I want to see a rainbow!”
But there was no rainbow.
“Where is it?” he asked.
Robo looked at the sky.
“We need something else.”
“What?”
“Sunlight!”
Just then, the clouds moved away.
The Sun came out.
Suddenly, a beautiful rainbow appeared.
“Wow!” shouted Milo.
He saw red.
Orange.
Yellow.
Green.
Blue.
Indigo.
And violet.
“How did all these colours appear?” asked Milo.
Robo said, “Sunlight goes through tiny drops of water.
The light then shows many colours.”
A cloud covered the Sun.
The rainbow disappeared.
Milo laughed.
“Now I know the rainbow's secret!”

The Five Gold Coins.
Milo had five gold coins.
He went to a toy shop.
He saw a big red car.
“I want it!”
The car cost five coins.
Robo asked, “Do you want to use all your coins?”
Milo stopped and thought.
Then he saw a small blue ball.
The ball cost two coins.
“I have an idea,” said Milo.
“I can buy the ball and save three coins.”
He bought the ball.
Then he put three coins into his money box.
CLINK!
CLINK!
CLINK!
A few days later, Milo got two more coins.
He counted his money.
“One, two, three, four, five!”
“I have five coins again!”
Robo smiled.
“That happened because you saved some money.”
Milo nodded.
“I can use some money now and save some for later.”
