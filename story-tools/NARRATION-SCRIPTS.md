# Narration scripts for Adobe Firefly — Generate speech

**How to use:** one mission per generation. Paste ONE block below, generate, download
the WAV, and name it exactly as shown. Do all seven.

**Settings** (match what you already had): voice **Kim** (Young Adult, Female),
English (US), speed **0.9x**. Kim is a good pick — young, warm, and clear for a
five-year-old.

**Why the words must not be edited:** these lines are copied character-for-character
from the story files. The word-highlighting is built by matching the audio back to
this exact text. Changing, adding or removing words will break the highlight.
Punctuation and CAPS are already shaped for expression — those are safe.

**Where to press "Add Pause":** at every line marked `⏸` below. A pause between
sentences is what makes it sound like storytelling instead of reading a list.
If that is too fiddly, skip it — the line breaks alone already give a small gap.
