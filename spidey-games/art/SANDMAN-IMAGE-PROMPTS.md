# Sandman — prompts for the extra poses

The game currently uses one picture, `sandman.png`, for every moment of the
fight, with CSS doing the work (it darkens him when he is soaked, shakes him
when he is hit, and puts stars or a raincloud over his head).

If you want him to actually change pose, generate the four below and drop
them in this folder with these exact names. The game will pick them up.

**Keep these identical in every image**, or he will look like four different
characters:

- Same man: dark brown short hair, stubble beard, olive-green short-sleeve
  work shirt, brown leather belt, dark brown work trousers, brown boots
- Same rule: his **left side is a real man**, his **right side dissolves into
  flowing golden-brown sand**
- Same camera: full body, side-on three-quarter view, facing LEFT
- Same background: **transparent** (no floor, no shadow, no scenery)
- Same lighting: warm daylight from the upper left
- Same framing: he fills the frame with a little empty space around him
- Square image, 1024×1024
